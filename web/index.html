MARKET DESIGN

Clarity starts here.

A good market describes one real world event, one city and a deadline. Participants can read the rules before putting test GEN behind their view.

What counts as YES?

The event physically begins with attendees in the specified city by the deadline. Cancellation, online only, another city or postponement beyond the deadline count as NO.
