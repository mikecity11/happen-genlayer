A clear question.

01

Set the rules

Name the event, city, UTC deadline, and two public evidence sources before anyone takes a position.

02

Pick a side

YES means the event began in the named city by the deadline. NO covers cancellation, an online-only event, or postponement beyond it.

03

Check the evidence

Validators independently read both sources and compare their decisions. Unclear evidence leaves the market unresolved.
